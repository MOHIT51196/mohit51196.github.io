Aura Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

The short version: Aura Studio processes everything on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Your audio never leaves your phone.

This Privacy Policy describes how Aura Studio ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App on Android or iOS. Aura Studio is an on-device audio editor: importing, processing, previewing, and exporting all happen locally on your device.

1. Data we collect

None. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. We operate no servers that receive data from the App.

  • Your audio and video files are read and processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, location data, usage statistics, or diagnostics.

2. Data stored on your device

To make editing sessions work, the App stores the following in its own private app storage, on your device only:

  • Session copies — a local copy of each imported file (audio only; the video portion of a video file is never stored), so a session survives the source file being moved or deleted.
  • Working files and exports — temporary render files and the audio files you export.
  • Settings — your effect settings per session and app preferences such as the theme.

This data stays under your control. Deleting a session in the App removes its stored copy, and uninstalling the App removes all of its data. Because we never receive your data, we cannot access, restore, or delete it on your behalf — there is nothing on our side to delete.

3. Purchases

The optional Aura Studio Pro subscription is processed entirely by Google Play (or the App Store, where applicable). We never receive your name, payment details, or billing address. The App stores only an anonymous entitlement record ("Pro is active until date X") in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) so your purchase works offline. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

  • Media access — files are opened only through the system file picker or when you explicitly share a file to the App. The App reads only the files you choose and requests no broad storage access.
  • The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or location.

5. Data sharing

We share no data with anyone, because we hold none. The App embeds no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The only third party involved in your use of the App is the store platform (Google Play / App Store) for installation and payments, under its own terms.

6. Security

Local-only processing is the strongest safeguard: your content is never exposed to a network service by the App. The purchase entitlement is kept in the platform's secure storage, and the App communicates with no server of ours.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and is suitable for a general audience. Since the App collects no data from any user, it also collects no data from children.

8. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA grant rights over personal data held by a company — access, correction, deletion, and portability. Aura Studio holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or erase. All of your content and settings live on your device, where you can review or delete them at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version ships, with the date above revised. Material changes will be summarized in the App's release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy are welcome at dev.mohitmalhotra@gmail.com, or through the developer contact listed on the App's Google Play page.
